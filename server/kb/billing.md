# Acme Cloud: Billing

> Fictional sample knowledge base for a made-up product ("Acme Cloud"). Not real documentation.

## Where can I download invoices?
Invoices are under Billing, then Invoices. Each one can be downloaded as a PDF. Only Owners can see the Billing page.

## What plans does Acme Cloud offer?
Acme Cloud has Free, Pro and Business plans. Free includes one project and community support. Pro adds unlimited projects and email support. Business adds single sign-on and a 99.9 percent uptime commitment.

## How do I cancel my subscription?
Owners can cancel under Billing, then Plan, then Cancel subscription. Access continues until the end of the paid period and no further charges are made. Refunds for disputed charges are handled by the billing team.
