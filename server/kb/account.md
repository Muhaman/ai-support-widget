# Acme Cloud: Account and access

> Fictional sample knowledge base for a made-up product ("Acme Cloud"). Not real documentation.

## How do I reset my password?
Open Settings, choose Security, then select Reset password. Acme Cloud emails a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and confirm the address on your account.

## How do I enable two-factor authentication?
Go to Settings, then Security, then Two-factor authentication. Scan the QR code with any authenticator app and enter the six digit code. Save the ten recovery codes somewhere safe; each works once.

## How do I invite a teammate?
Open Team, choose Invite member and enter their email address. Invitations expire after seven days. Owners and Admins can invite; Members cannot.
